KEY FINDING
========================================================================
Both models confuse Blackhole and Version attacks with each other far more
than any other pair of classes. Gradient Boosting's higher overall accuracy
comes from trading Random Forest's weak Blackhole recall (0.32) for much
better Blackhole recall (0.69) -- at the cost of Version recall dropping
from 0.72 to 0.29. This is a data-separability limitation (Blackhole and
Version messages look very similar in the available features), not
something either model architecture fixes.
